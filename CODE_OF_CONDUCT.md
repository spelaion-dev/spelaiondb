# Code of conduct

SpelaionDB follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/):
we want taking part in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever
their background, identity or level of experience.

In short:

- Be kind and assume good intent; disagree about ideas, never about people.
- Give and take feedback gracefully: everyone here is learning.
- No harassment, insults, sexualised language or imagery, or publishing others' private
  information.

It applies in the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions) and wherever someone
represents the project.

## Reporting
Write to **contact@spelaion.dev**. Reports are read only by the maintainers and kept confidential.
The maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributions that break this code, and
temporarily or permanently ban those who do, as the Contributor Covenant's enforcement
guidelines describe.
